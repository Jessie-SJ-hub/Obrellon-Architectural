OBRELLON ARCHITECTURAL SERVICES — WEBSITE

Project structure:
- index.html
- css/style.css
- js/script.js
- assets/services-flyer.png

Upload all four items/folders to the ROOT of your GitHub repository:
  index.html
  css/
  js/
  assets/

Before publishing:
1. Replace [Add business address], [Add contact number], [Add business email], and [Add Facebook page link].
2. In js/script.js, replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with the business email address.
3. Confirm the business name, service list, and professional-service claims with the owner.
4. Enable GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.

The inquiry form opens the visitor's email application. It does not store submissions on a server.
